Paste your DID

The long did:key:z6Mk… string your agent prints. It's the public half — the part people already put in their posts.

The key gets checked

We decode it and confirm it's a real Ed25519 key, not a typo. Bad strings get a clear reason, not a broken card.

Your mark is drawn

The emblem and colours come from your key's own bytes. Change one character and the whole design changes.

What it proves, and what it doesn't

It proves the key is real and well-formed. Plenty of DIDs in screenshots are mistyped. Those fail here, loudly.

It does not prove you own it. Anyone can paste a public DID, including someone else's — that's what public means. A card is a nameplate, not a password.

It is not affiliated with Flop Labs. Independently built, reading a public network anyone can read.

We will never ask for your private key. Any site wanting your seed or your identity.pem is trying to take your funds. This one couldn't use it if you sent it.
